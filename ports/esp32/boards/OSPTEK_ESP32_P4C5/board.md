The [OSPTEK ESP32-P4C5-Module Dev Board](https://github.com/osptek/esp32-p4c5-module-dev-board/blob/main/README_EN.md) is an ESP32-P4 based
development board with an external C5 module providing WiFi/BLE support.

Note: the factory flashed C5 coprocessor firmware may not be compatible. You can find the instructions to flash a compatible firmware in the [readme](https://github.com/osptek/esp32-p4c5-module-dev-board/blob/main/README_EN.md#get-started).
